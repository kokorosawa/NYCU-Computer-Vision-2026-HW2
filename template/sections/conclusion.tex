\section{Conclusion}
This project established a complete object-detection pipeline with reproducible training, evaluation, and inference.
I also note an early misunderstanding: at first I interpreted the homework as allowing pretrained transformer weights.
After clarifying the requirement, I used the correct setup where only the ResNet-50 backbone is pretrained.

Using that early DETR run (\texttt{devout-cosmos-42 / xrob5wme}) as baseline, convergence is very slow and AP remains very low (latest AP=0.00003, best AP=0.00039).
After correcting the setup and migrating to Deformable DETR, AP improves substantially to 0.2993 (checkpoint-backed in \texttt{kqscm42c}).
The successful Deformable run also shows stable epoch-wise improvement.

Future work will focus on longer successful Deformable DETR training and stronger augmentation/scheduler ablations under the same homework-compliant setting.
