\subsection{Key Findings}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The compliant DETR run (\texttt{xrob5wme}) converged very slowly:} after 21 epochs, AP remained at 0.00039.
    \item \textbf{Successful Deformable DETR training improved substantially over the compliant DETR baseline:} AP=0.2993 and AP50=0.5785 in run \texttt{kqscm42c}.
    \item \textbf{Within the successful run, learning is stable:} AP rises from 0.0329 (epoch 1) to 0.2993 (epoch 13).
\end{enumerate}
